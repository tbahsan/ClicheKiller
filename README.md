## 💡 What is ClicheKiller?

George Orwell famously wrote in *Politics and the English Language*:
> *"Never use a metaphor, simile, or other figure of speech which you are used to seeing in print."*

When writers rely on pre-packaged expressions like *"at the end of the day"*, *"cold sweat"*, or *"low-hanging fruit"*, prose loses vividness and authenticity. 

**ClicheKiller** is a modern, privacy-first editor that scans drafts in real time for overused clichés, melodramatic tropes, and corporate buzzwords — and offers **1-click punchy, concrete alternatives** to sharpen your prose instantly.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **1-Click Rewriting:** Click any highlighted cliché in the editor to see why it's weary and swap it instantly with a fresh, punchy alternative.
- 📚 **148 Curated Clichés:** Covers Dead Metaphors, Fiction Melodrama, Corporate Jargon, and Puffed Filler Phrases with human-crafted explanations and alternatives.
- ⚡ **100% Client-Side & Offline PWA:** Instant loading backed by a Service Worker precache. Runs locally without an internet connection.
- 🔒 **Zero Surveillance:** No server backends, no tracking analytics, no user accounts. Your text never leaves your device.
- 🎚️ **Category Filter Toggles:** Focus on what matters to your genre — toggle fiction tropes off for technical reports, or disable business buzzwords for literary fiction.
- 📊 **Prose Health & Coverage:** Calculates exact cliché word density without moralizing writing quality scores.
- 🌓 **Dark & Light Mode:** High-contrast, distraction-free visual design.
- 📋 **Export Ready:** One-click clean text copy or UTF-8 `.txt` file export.

## 🗂️ Cliché Categories

| Category / বিভাগ | Description / বিবরণ | Examples / উদাহরণ |
|---|---|---|
| **Dead Metaphors (মৃত রূপক)** | Metaphors so overused they no longer evoke imagery. | *at the end of the day*, *tip of the iceberg*, *bite the bullet*, *thinking outside the box* |
| **Fiction Tropes (কল্পকাহিনির ক্লিশে)** | Melodramatic bodily reactions and stock novel shortcuts. | *cold sweat*, *heart sank*, *blood ran cold*, *breath he didn't know he was holding* |
| **Corporate Jargon (করপোরেট বুলি)** | Vague, inflated office buzzwords that mask simple meanings. | *low-hanging fruit*, *circle back*, *touch base*, *synergy*, *move the needle* |
| **Filler Phrases (অতিরিক্ত ফুলানো কথা)** | Bloated multi-word phrases that can be simplified or deleted. | *in this day and age* (now), *for all intents and purposes* (essentially), *needless to say* |

## 🇧🇩 বাংলা বিবরণ

**ClicheKiller** হলো লেখায় অতিরিক্ত ব্যবহৃত বা ক্লিশে (cliché) শব্দগুচ্ছ খুঁজে বের করে সেগুলোকে আরও শক্তিশালী ও আকর্ষণীয় শব্দে রূপান্তর করার একটি আধুনিক ডিজিটাল সহকারী।

উপন্যাস, গল্প, প্রবন্ধ, প্রাতিষ্ঠানিক প্রতিবেদন বা ইমেইল লেখার সময় আমরা অজান্তেই বহু ব্যবহৃত গতানুগতিক শব্দগুচ্ছ ব্যবহার করে ফেলি। এই টুলটি:
- আপনার লেখার ক্লিশে অংশগুলো আলাদা রঙে মার্ক করে দেয়।
- ক্লিশেটিতে ক্লিক করলেই একটি কার্ড ভেসে ওঠে, যাতে কেন এটি ক্লিশে এবং এর বদলে কোন চমৎকার বিকল্প শব্দ ব্যবহার করা যায় তা দেখায়।
- মাত্র এক ক্লিকেই ক্লিশেটি বদলে নতুন বিকল্প শব্দ বসে যায়!

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

```text
src/
├── engine/              # Pure TypeScript phrase matching engine (Zero DOM dependencies)
│   ├── types.ts         # Type definitions & data interfaces
│   ├── tokenizer.ts     # Word tokenization & exact character offset mapping
│   ├── matcher.ts       # N-gram matching & longest-match overlap resolution
│   ├── replacer.ts      # Capitalization-preserving substring replacement
│   └── index.ts         # Unified engine exports
├── data/
│   └── cliches.json     # 148 curated clichés with explanations & alternatives
├── storage/
│   └── draft-store.ts   # Opt-in client-side LocalStorage manager
└── main.ts              # Synchronized backdrop highlighter & UI controller
```

- **Runtime:** Native ES2022 + Vanilla DOM (Fast, lightweight, no framework bloat)
- **Bundler:** Vite
- **Testing:** Vitest (12 Unit tests) + Playwright (5 Cross-browser E2E tests)
- **Offline:** Workbox PWA Service Worker
- **Deployment:** GitHub Pages via GitHub Actions
